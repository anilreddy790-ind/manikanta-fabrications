WHAT WE DO

Welding & fabrication services

We provide custom welding and fabrication services for gates, grills, railings, shutters, sheds and metal structures for homes, shops and commercial properties. Every project is fabricated to your measurements, design and requirements, with a focus on strong welding, practical construction and neat finishing.

01

Gates

Custom main gates, entrance gates and Metal gates designed to suit your property, available space and preferred style.

02

Grills

Window, balcony and safety grills fabricated to size for homes, shops and other properties, with practical and neat designs.

03

Railings

Staircase, balcony and terrace railings fabricated to measurements, combining everyday safety with a clean finished appearance.

04

Shutters

Rolling shutter fabrication and related metalwork for shops, commercial spaces and other applications requiring secure access.

05

Sheds & Structures

Metal shed and structural fabrication for homes, shops and commercial requirements, planned around the available space and intended use.

Custom Welding

HOW IT WORKS

Simple from enquiry to installation.

From understanding your requirement and taking measurements to fabrication, finishing and installation, we handle each stage with care to deliver practical metalwork for your property.

01

Discuss

Tell us about your requirement, preferred design, purpose and budget.

02

Measure

Confirm site dimensions, available space and installation requirements.

03

Plan & Design

Discuss the suitable design, dimensions, materials and fabrication details.

04

Material Preparation

Cut, prepare and arrange the required pipes, rods, sheets, angles and metal sections.

05

Fabricate

Assemble and weld the metalwork according to the agreed measurements and design.

06

Finish

Complete grinding, surface preparation and finishing work for a clean appearance.

07

Install

Position, fit and install the finished metalwork at your site.

08

Final Check

Check alignment, fit and finishing before completing the installation.
